Our Story

Om Shree Art was born in the heart of Kathmandu with a simple mission — to make professional printing accessible to every business, big or small. What started as a small stamp-making shop has grown into a full-service printing studio trusted by thousands of clients across Nepal.

With over 12 years of experience, we've printed everything from single business cards to massive flex hoardings, from custom rubber stamps to illuminated LED sign boards. Every order — big or small — gets the same level of dedication and precision.

We believe great printing tells a story. And we're here to help you tell yours.
