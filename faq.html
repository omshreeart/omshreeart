📦 Orders & Delivery

You can place an order by contacting us on WhatsApp (+977 9851154009), calling us directly, or visiting our studio in Kathmandu. We'll guide you through design, size, and material selection.

We offer same-day delivery across the Kathmandu valley for most standard orders placed before 12 PM. Custom or bulk orders may take 1–3 business days. We'll confirm the timeline when you place your order.

Yes! We ship across Nepal via courier services. Delivery time outside the valley is typically 2–5 business days depending on your location. Extra shipping charges apply.

Most of our products have no minimum order — you can order even a single piece. However, bulk orders of 50+ units receive special discounted pricing. Contact us for bulk quotes.

🎨 Design & Files

Yes! We have an in-house design team. If you don't have a ready design, we can create one for you. Basic design is free for orders above Rs. 1,000. Complex designs may have a small design fee.

We accept PDF, AI, PSD, CDR, PNG, JPG files. For best print quality, please provide files at 300 DPI or higher in CMYK color mode. We'll let you know if any adjustments are needed.

Absolutely! We send a digital proof via WhatsApp for your approval before starting production. We never print without your confirmation on the final design.

💳 Payment & Pricing

We accept cash, eSewa, Khalti, bank transfer, and QR payment. Payment can be made at delivery or in advance. For large orders, a 50% advance may be required.

Yes! We offer attractive discounts for bulk orders. The more you order, the more you save. Contact us on WhatsApp with your requirements and we'll give you a custom quote with the best pricing.

If there is a printing error on our part, we will reprint or refund at no cost. However, once you approve the design proof, we cannot accept returns for design-related issues. Please review your proof carefully before approving.

🖨️ Products & Quality

We use UV printing, digital offset, large-format solvent printing, and laser engraving depending on the product. All our machines are professional-grade for sharp, long-lasting results.

Our outdoor prints (flex, vinyl, sign boards) are UV-resistant and last 3–5 years outdoors. Indoor prints and business cards last indefinitely with normal use. Rubber stamps last thousands of impressions.

Yes, we can print a sample for large orders. A sample fee applies which is adjusted against your final order amount. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
